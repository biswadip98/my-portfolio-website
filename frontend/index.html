About me

A solution consultant growing into DevOps and platform engineering.

I work as a Senior Consultant & Solution Engineer at Techno Exponent, where my job is to understand what a business actually needs and turn it into reliable, production-ready software. Consulting keeps me close to the whole delivery picture - requirements, stakeholders, architecture and the realities of shipping to production - not just one slice of it.

I'm channelling that end-to-end view into DevOps and platform engineering. The same instinct that makes a good solution engineer - seeing how every piece connects and where delivery breaks down - is exactly what CI/CD pipelines, GitOps and infrastructure automation are built to solve. I've been putting it into practice hands-on: automated pipelines, GitOps delivery with Argo CD, containerised workloads on Kubernetes, and infrastructure as code.

My start in software - an engineering internship, then mobile application development - means I understand both sides of the wall: the code that gets shipped and the pipelines and infrastructure that ship it. I care about clean pipelines, reproducible infrastructure, and secure-by-default delivery.
